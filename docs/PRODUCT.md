# INSYT. — Product Definition

> AI-powered personal finance coaching. Calm. Intelligent. Actionable.

---

## What is INSYT.?

INSYT. is a personal finance intelligence platform that acts as a calm, non-judgemental financial coach. It helps everyday people understand where their money is going, reduce debt intelligently, build savings habits, and feel genuinely in control of their financial life — without spreadsheets, jargon, or anxiety.

The AI coach doesn't just report numbers. It interprets them, contextualises them, and delivers personalised guidance the way a trusted advisor would: clearly, warmly, and with genuine insight.

---

## Product Vision

> "Make financial clarity feel effortless and financial progress feel inevitable."

INSYT. exists to close the gap between what people know about their finances (very little) and what they _need_ to know to improve them. Most financial tools show data. INSYT. explains what that data _means_ and what to _do_ about it.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary**: UK-based adults aged 25–45 who:

- Earn a moderate-to-good income but feel financially anxious
- Have some debt (credit cards, loans, buy-now-pay-later)
- Want to save but don't know where to start
- Find traditional budgeting tools overwhelming or clinical
- Are comfortable with modern apps and AI-assisted products

**Secondary**: Anyone who wants a smarter, calmer relationship with money — regardless of income level.

**Non-audience**: Institutional investors, financial advisors, accountants. INSYT. is personal, not professional.

---

## App Goals

1. **Clarity** — Give users an instant, honest picture of their financial health
2. **Actionability** — Every insight should suggest a clear next step
3. **Progress** — Show users they are moving forward, even in small ways
4. **Habit formation** — Reward regular check-ins and consistent behaviour
5. **Emotional safety** — Make money feel manageable, not shameful

---

## Feature Philosophy

Features are added only if they:

1. Reduce financial anxiety or increase financial clarity
2. Can be understood without reading documentation
3. Feel like they belong in a premium modern product
4. Are better than the equivalent competitor experience

**Always ask before building a feature:**

- Does this make the user feel smarter about their money?
- Does this reduce friction or add it?
- Would a Monzo/Linear designer approve this?

## What INSYT. Is

- A calm, intelligent financial companion
- A non-judgemental coach that meets users where they are
- A data-driven product with a human-centred voice
- A premium experience at a consumer price point
- A product that respects user attention and doesn't waste it

---

## What INSYT. Is NOT

- A bank account or payment product
- A regulated financial advisor
- A spreadsheet replacement
- A data-heavy dashboard with no interpretation
- A product that shames users for their financial situation
- A product cluttered with unnecessary features
- A product that requires expertise to use

---

## Brand Voice (Summary)

- **Tone**: Calm, clear, confident, warm
- **Personality**: Like a brilliant friend who happens to understand finance
- **Avoid**: Jargon, guilt, urgency, hype, generic platitudes
- **Use**: Specificity, encouragement, clarity, actionability

---

## Success Metrics

- User returns within 7 days of first session
- User adds at least 3 data categories in first session
- AI insights are rated helpful >80% of the time
- Financial Health Score improves for engaged users over 90 days
- Zero churn from "it was confusing" or "I didn't understand it"
